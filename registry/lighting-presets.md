# REGISTRY: lighting-presets
# Ready-made lighting styles to reference in any skill

## chiaroscuro-low-key
Dark moody cinematic. Single narrow directional beam.
Sharp highlights, deep shadows. Completely black abstract background.
Caravaggio-grade. No fill light. Maximum contrast.
Keep faces original.

## golden-hour-epic
Peter Jackson cinematography. High-contrast golden hour.
Atmospheric God-rays. Naturalistic light-wrap.
Kodak Vision3 50D film emulation. Deep saturation, organic grain.

## cold-steel-blue
Cold_Steel_Blue LUT. S-Curve aggressive contrast.
Glacial White highlights. Deep Void Blue shadows.
Temperature consistency across all frames (Itten Contrast).

## flat-studio-technical
Bright, even, functional fluorescent and LED lighting.
No harsh shadows. Clinical and sterile. Cool white light tone.
Zero film grain, zero dramatic lighting. Deep DOF, ultra-sharp.
