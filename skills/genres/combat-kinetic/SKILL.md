# SKILL: combat-kinetic
# Category: genre
# Trigger: fight scene, impact photography, kinetic violence

## Visual Philosophy
Neo-Noir Brutalism, Caravaggio-grade lighting
Texture of rust, sweat, and shattered glass

## Color Grade
- Steel-Blue Shadows vs Ember-Orange Highlights
- Hard Rim Lighting (10/10 intensity)
- Volumetric dust motes in light beams
- Skin: hyper-detailed pores, salt-stains, capillary redness

## Composition Logic
Golden Spiral leading to the fist/impact point in every frame

## Protagonist: The Obsidian Engine
- scars, sweat-soaked tactical gear, predatory focus
- veins like cables

## Antagonist: The Shattered Icon
- elegant features distorting, airborne, disintegrating composure

## Impact Rules
- micro-debris orbits impact point like Saturn's rings
- shockwave visible traveling through muscle
- heat haze and pressure distortion around fist
- wall tiles cracking from air pressure alone

## Strict Rules
- THE FIST: sharpest object in frame, zero motion blur
- concrete: pulverized to fine powder, not just cracked
- clothing: stretched to point of micro-tearing on back
