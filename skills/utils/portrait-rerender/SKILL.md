# SKILL: portrait-rerender
# Category: utility
# Trigger: upgrade portrait to Sony A1 quality

## Camera Spec (mandatory)
- Camera: Sony A1 | Lens: 85mm f/1.4 | Aperture: f/1.6
- ISO: 100 | Shutter: 1/200s
- Shallow cinematic depth of field, editorial-neutral color profile

## Light Upgrade
- soft directional light, warm highlights, cool shadows
- deeper contrast, expanded dynamic range
- micro-contrast boost, zero harsh shadows

## Preserve Absolutely
- subject identity, facial geometry
- original background, pose, expression

## Output
- 4K, 10-bit, cinematic editorial style
- real skin texture, subtle film grain

## Forbidden
- new background, face morphing, fake glow
- flat lighting, over-smooth skin
