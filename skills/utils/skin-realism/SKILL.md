# SKILL: skin-realism
# Category: utility
# Trigger: portrait enhancement, skin detail

## Apply
- visible pores, skin texture, micro-details
- small natural imperfections
- flyaway hairs, baby hairs catching light
- subsurface scattering, light glowing through skin edges
- subtle eye moisture (not tears)
- organic texture, ultra-realistic finish

## Preserve
- all facial features and expressions
- identity — no face changes

## Forbidden
- plastic skin, beauty filter smoothing
- digital glow, fake moisture
- uniform skin tone
