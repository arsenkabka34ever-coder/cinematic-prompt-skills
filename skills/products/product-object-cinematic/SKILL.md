## Philosophy: Industrial Purity, Survivalist Luxury, Hyper-Haptic Coldness

## Color Grade
- base_lut: Cold_Steel_Blue
- contrast: S-Curve_Aggressive
- highlights: Glacial White (R:240, G:250, B:255)
- shadows: Deep Void Blue (R:5, G:10, B:20)

## Light Logic
- expose for highlights, crush the blacks
- rim: sharp 1px, no soft glow
- depth: foreground droplets diamond sharp, background infinite blur

## Shot Arc (9 frames)
1. Extreme wide low angle — object on concrete monolith
2. Macro condensation — droplets like sweat, skin-texture realism
3. Top down — open neck, water surface reflection
4. Profile silhouette — rim light only, total blackness
5. Action insert — cap being twisted, kinetic energy
6. Physics simulation — water slow motion, bubbles suspended
7. Atmospheric — steam/fog environment
8. Abstract texture — light caustic patterns
9. Human hand reaching for object — veins, desperate grasp

## Rules
- object must look expensive by purity, not brand
- lighting must evoke thirst/desire
- no background distractions — only texture and void
